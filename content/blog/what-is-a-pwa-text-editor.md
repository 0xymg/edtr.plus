You probably use apps every day. Some you download from the App Store or Google Play. Some you open in your browser. But there is a third kind of app that many people do not know about: the **Progressive Web App**, or **PWA**.

A PWA is a website that acts like a real app. You can install it on your computer or phone. It works offline. It opens in its own window. But you never visit an app store.

EDTR is a PWA. And this is one of the reasons it is so useful.

## How Does a PWA Work?

A PWA uses modern web technology to give you an app-like experience. Here is what happens:

1. You visit a website (like edtr.cc)
2. Your browser asks if you want to install it
3. You click "Install"
4. The app appears on your desktop or home screen
5. It opens in its own window, without browser tabs or address bar

That is it. No download from an app store. No big file to install. No updates to manage.

## Why Is This Good for a Text Editor?

A text editor is something you use often. Maybe every day. You want it to be fast and always ready. A PWA gives you exactly that.

### Always Available

Traditional apps can be hundreds of megabytes. EDTR as a PWA is very small. It takes almost no space on your device. You will not even notice it.

## EDTR PWA Features

When you install EDTR as a PWA, you get some extra features:

### Open Files Directly

On supported browsers (Chrome and Edge), you can right-click a text file on your computer and choose "Open with EDTR." The file opens right in the editor. EDTR supports many file types: `.txt`, `.js`, `.py`, `.html`, `.css`, `.json`, `.md`, and more.

### Desktop Integration

EDTR appears in your taskbar or dock like any other app. You can pin it, add it to your start menu, and switch to it with Alt+Tab.

### Your Own Window

Unlike a browser tab, EDTR as a PWA gets its own clean window. No distracting bookmarks bar. No other tabs. Just your text editor.

## PWA vs Native App vs Website

| Feature | Website | PWA (EDTR) | Native App |
|---------|---------|------------|------------|
| Works offline | No | Yes | Yes |
| Install from app store | No | No | Yes |
| Own window | No | Yes | Yes |

| Uses much storage | No | No | Often yes |

As you can see, a PWA gives you the best of both worlds.
